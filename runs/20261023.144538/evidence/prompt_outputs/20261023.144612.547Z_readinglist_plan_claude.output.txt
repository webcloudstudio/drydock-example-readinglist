## story reject-empty-title
summary:      Reject a submission whose title is empty and report the reason.
type:         service
kind:         capability
phase:        2
implements:   FEATURE-RejectEmptyTitle.md
covers:       VALID-001
accepts:      st-005
context:      DATABASE.md
stack:        python.md, flask.md
consumes:     POST /books
depends:      add-book
acceptance:   yes
instructions: |
  In the POST /books path, reject a missing, empty, or whitespace-only title with status 400 and
  a non-empty reason. When the request sends Accept: application/json the body is JSON with
  "error" (the reason) and "field" equal to "title"; otherwise the page is re-rendered with the
  reason shown. Nothing is stored on rejection.
