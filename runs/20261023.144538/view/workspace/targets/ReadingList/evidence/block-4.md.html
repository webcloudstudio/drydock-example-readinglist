What I built

  • app_server/book_service.py: add_book(database, title, author) stores through db.books.create. It has no Flask dependency and refuses an empty title or author with a reason, before touching the store.
  • app_server/books.py: the books blueprint with POST /books. It trims title and author, then answers 303 to / on success. On rejection it answers 400 and puts the reason in the base.html alert region.
  • app_server/__init__.py: registers the blueprint in create_app().
  • tests/test_add_book.py: 25 tests on a temporary store, covering the redirect, storage read back through the persistence package, trimming, append order, the rejection cases, and the service. Each test uses its own store, so none touch data/app.db.
  • tests/test_create_app_wiring.py: I changed test_create_app_registers_no_book_routes to expect books.create_book beside static, since adding the route made the old assertion false.
